VAPOR BOI

Deliver. Throw. Repeat.

Level Design Level Implementation Game Design
// project_overview

Vapor Boi is a VR newspaper delivery game that plays like frisbee golf. Grab a stack of papers, arc your throws, and nail every porch on the block.

Vapor Boi was inspired by my real life experience delivering newspapers in Denver and Seattle. The levels are inspired by real houses that I delivered to, complete with inconvenient bushes, flower beds, and delicate lawn ornaments.

We created this for the 2023 VR Game Jam. The theme was "Special Delivery". The idea of a frisbee golf newspaper delivery game had been knocking around in my head ever since I started delivering newspapers in Denver in 2014.

We ended up completing 4 levels, but had plans for several more. To complete a level, you must deliver the newspaper to the scoring zone on the house's doormat.

The scoring system was not implemented in time, but the intention was that there would be a stroke counter, which tracked how many times you threw the paper before getting it into the scoring zone. Flower beds, windows and lawn ornaments are supposed to cause penalties if hit.

Someday, I intend to return to this concept and make it into one large map rather than separated levels. I want to add driving mechanics, scoring, timing, and a vehicle upgrade system to make this into a fully fledged game.

// location: THE_ARCH — 01

[ The Arch — View 1 ]

The Arch is based on a real location. The difficulty of this house is that you have a narrow vertical window to throw the paper under the hedge arch, but above the gate. The doormat target is on a small set of stairs, and to either side are bushes that the paper can get lost in, and windows that can be smashed.
